Probleem

Info on kuskil olemas. Masin ei leia seda üles või saab valesti aru.

  • AI ei tea, millises piirkonnas sa töid teed.
  • Veebilehel on üks telefoninumber, kataloogis ja Facebookis teine.
  • Teenuse kirjeldus ei ütle, milliseid seadmeid sa paigaldad või hooldad.
  • Hinnakiri või teenuste loetelu on ainult PDF-is või sügaval lehe sees.
  • Veebileht on inimesele loetav, aga masinale ei ütle see selgelt, kes sa oled ja mida teed.

Mis on masinloetav teenuseprofiil?

Struktureeritud kirjeldus sellest, mida sa pakud, kus tegutsed, kellele teenus sobib, millised on tingimused ja kuidas sinuga ühendust võtta. Sama info on kirjas nii, et inimene saab seda lugeda ja masin aru saada.

JSON-LD ja teised tehnilised failid on selle osa, mitte iseseisev lahendus. Need aitavad ainult siis, kui info ise on õige ja kõikjal ühesugune.

Korduma kippuvad küsimused

Küsimused

Kas see tähendab, et ChatGPT hakkab minu ettevõtet soovitama?

Ei pruugi. AI-assistent otsustab ise, keda nimetada, ja tema vastused muutuvad. Seda ei saa keegi lubada. Saan teha nii, et sinu kohta käiv info oleks õige, kõikjal ühesugune ja masinale arusaadav. Siis on suurem võimalus, et kui sind mainitakse, tehakse seda õigesti.

Milliseid AI-assistente kontrollite?

Kolme levinumat üldkasutatavat assistenti [kinnitamata: nt ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity]. Kasutan neid nii, nagu klient neid kasutab, ilma isikupärastamiseta. Raportis on kirjas, millist assistenti ja millal kasutasin.

Kas teenus sobib väikesele ettevõttele?

Jah, see on tehtud just väikestele ettevõtetele, kes pakuvad selgeid teenuseid kindlas piirkonnas. Piisab, kui sul on veebileht või vähemalt mõni avalik kirje, näiteks Google'i ettevõtteprofiil.

Kas peate muutma minu veebilehte?

Mitte alati. Sageli on suurem mõju kataloogides ja Google'i ettevõtteprofiilis olevate andmete parandamisel. Kui veebilehte on vaja muuta, kirjutan täpse muudatuse ette. Kes selle teeb, lepime kokku.

Kas kontrollite ka Google'i, Facebooki ja ettevõtete kataloogide infot?

Jah. Infoauditis vaatan läbi sinu veebilehe, Google'i ettevõtteprofiili, Facebooki lehe ja kataloogid, kus ettevõte on kirjas. Paranduste nimekirjas on kirjas, mida ja kus muuta. Ilma kokkuleppeta ma sinu eest midagi ei muuda.

Mida ma pärast teenuse ostmist kätte saan?

Algmõõtmise raporti, info auditi, paranduste nimekirja, korrastatud ettevõtte ja teenuste profiili, vajadusel masinloetavad failid sinu veebilehe jaoks, tehtud muudatuste kokkuvõtte ja kordusmõõtmise raporti. Failid jäävad sulle.

Kas AI-nähtavust saab regulaarselt mõõta?

Jah, samu küsimusi saab korrata ja tulemusi võrrelda. Püsiteenusena ma seda praegu ei paku. Kui soovid pärast pilooti korduvat mõõtmist, lepime selle eraldi kokku.

The problem

The information exists somewhere. A machine can’t find it, or gets it wrong.

  • AI doesn’t know which area you work in.
  • Your website shows one phone number, a directory and Facebook show another.
  • Your service description doesn’t say which equipment you install or maintain.
  • Your price list or list of services is only in a PDF or buried deep in the site.
  • Your website is readable for people, but doesn’t clearly tell a machine who you are and what you do.

What I check

Four levels

  1. AI visibilityDoes an AI assistant find your company and name it when a customer asks a relevant question?
  2. Reliable informationAre your name, contacts, services and area correct and consistent across sources?
  3. Agent readinessCan a machine understand your services, terms, limits and the next step?
  4. Agent transaction readinessAn AI agent asks you for a quote or a time slot on a customer’s behalf. This is a future direction, not a promise of the current service.Direction

The current service covers levels 1–3.

How it works

Measure, check, fix, measure again

  1. MeasureI ask several AI assistants realistic customer questions about your company and record the answers and sources.
  2. CheckI compare the AI answers with your confirmed data and with public sources.
  3. FixI help put your service descriptions, contact details, areas and technical presentation in order.
  4. Measure againI repeat the same questions and compare the results.

AI answers vary over time and between platforms. Measuring again shows what changed, but doesn’t guarantee the result you want.

What you get

Concrete deliverables

  1. Baseline measurement report
  2. Audit of your public company information
  3. List of gaps and fixes
  4. Clean company and service profile
  5. JSON-LD and other machine-readable files, where needed
  6. Summary of the changes made
  7. Follow-up measurement report
What is a machine-readable service profile?

A structured description of what you offer, where you work, who the service is for, what the terms are and how to contact you. The same information is written so that a person can read it and a machine can understand it.

JSON-LD and other technical files are part of this, not a solution on their own. They only help when the information itself is correct and consistent everywhere.

Method

Five questions you can repeat

For every company I use the same five types of question. I fill in the parts in brackets with your details.

  1. Brand awarenessWhat do you know about the company [name]?
  2. Category visibilityWhich [service] provider would you recommend in [city]?
  3. Problem-led discoveryWho should I contact if I have [problem] in [location]?
  4. ExpertiseWhat should a customer consider when choosing [service]?
  5. Service accuracyDoes [name] offer [service] in [area], and how do I contact them?

Frequently asked questions

Questions

Does this mean ChatGPT will start recommending my company?

Not necessarily. An AI assistant decides on its own whom to name, and its answers change. Nobody can promise that. What I can do is make sure the information about you is correct, consistent everywhere and understandable to a machine. Then, if you are mentioned, it is more likely to be done correctly.

Which AI assistants do you check?

Three widely used general assistants [to confirm: e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity]. I use them the way a customer would, without personalisation. The report states which assistant I used and when.

Is this suitable for a small company?

Yes, it is made for small companies that offer clear services in a specific area. A website or at least one public listing, such as a Google Business Profile, is enough.

Do you have to change my website?

Not always. Fixing the data in directories and in your Google Business Profile often matters more. If the website needs changes, I write down the exact change. We agree on who makes it.

Do you also check Google, Facebook and business directories?

Yes. The information audit covers your website, Google Business Profile, Facebook page and the directories where your company is listed. The list of fixes says what to change and where. I don’t change anything on your behalf without agreement.

How long does it take?

What do I get after buying the service?
