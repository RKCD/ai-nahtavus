Kuidas tulemust hiljem kontrollitakse

Kordusmõõtmine tehakse samade kliendiküsimuste ja samade assistentidega. Raportis on kirjas mõlema mõõtmise kuupäev, et oleks näha, mis muutus. Soovi korral võib mõõtmist hiljem korrata.

Piirangud
  • AI vastused muutuvad ajas ja platvormiti.
  • Mõõtmine on proovivalim, mitte statistiliselt esinduslik tulemus.
  • Muutus võib tuleneda ka assistendi enda muudatustest, mitte ainult parandustest.

Päris raportis on lisaks assistentide nimed, kuupäevad, allikad ja vastuste väljavõtted. Teiste ettevõtete nimesid ei avaldata.

AI visibility pilot: Näidisküte OÜ

Company
Näidisküte OÜ (fictional)

Measurements
Baseline 3 March · follow-up 14 April · three AI assistants

Summary

  • AI assistants knew little about the company and gave partly wrong information.
  • The most costly error: an old phone number in a directory, which one assistant passed on to the customer.
  • After the fixes the information was more accurate. The company was still not mentioned in recommendations for the area.

What I found, why it matters and what I recommend

FindingWhy it mattersRecommendation
Old phone number in a directoryCustomers call the wrong number and the work doesn’t come in.Fix the number in the directory. Check the other listings.
Service area not stated on the websiteAI offers the company only in Tartu city, not the county.State on the website, as text, which municipalities you cover.
Heat pump maintenance only in a PDF price listMachines don’t link the service to the company.A separate section on the website with the service and equipment types.
Opening hours differ between Google profile and websiteCustomers don’t know when to call.Align the opening hours.

The accuracy of the information improved. The company was still not mentioned in recommendations for the area after the fixes. Fixes cannot guarantee that.

How the result is checked later

The follow-up uses the same customer questions and the same assistants. The report shows the dates of both measurements, so you can see what changed. The measurement can be repeated later on request.

Limits
  • AI answers change over time and between platforms.
  • A measurement is a sample, not a statistically representative result.
  • A change can also come from the assistant itself, not only from the fixes.

A real report also names the assistants and includes dates, sources and excerpts from the answers. Names of other companies are not published.
